# Scoring implementation and open integrations

`contracts/arena/src/scoring.rs` holds exact arithmetic: signed rational scores compared by 512-bit cross multiplication, never floating point. Price gain and market-cap growth reject nonpositive start or end values, volume ratio divides by the initial market cap and rejects a zero denominator, and maximum drawdown rejects nonpositive prices. Round volume is the checked sum over a half-open `[t0, t1)` window; a trade at `t0` counts, one at `t1` invalidates the set, duplicate or blank trade IDs and `u128` overflow are rejected, and an empty complete set is a valid zero.

`contracts/arena/src/rounds.rs` is a pure state machine. A round window is recorded once by `open_round` and is not changed by later calls or by when resolution runs. `resolve_round` needs observations for exactly the current candidates, each bound to battle, round, asset, source, policy hash, the recorded window and a non-empty evidence hash, with strictly increasing times and boundary coverage. Drawdown additionally needs one ordered price per observation time and the frozen maximum gap. Anything missing, conflicting, out of order, of the wrong mode or unscorable returns `Pending` and records nothing. Tied leaders become the only candidates, the round number advances and a fresh window must start at or after the previous end; eliminated communities cannot re-enter, there is no round cap, and resolving a recorded round again returns the recorded outcome. The observation policy is committed without changing lifecycle's `ObservationPolicy` shape: `ObservationTerms` (source, quote currency, eligible pools, finality label, price/supply/volume decimals, boundary tolerance and maximum sample gap) hashes canonically with SHA-256 over length-prefixed fields, sorted pools and the scoring mode, and that digest is what the lock freezes. `Rounds::from_frozen_policy` is the only production constructor and refuses terms that do not hash to the frozen digest, incomplete terms, or (for the volume mode) a volume scale other than price plus supply decimals. No values are chosen here; provider, pools, cadence and tolerance remain OPEN. Freshness is expressed by the boundary tolerance, since a late resolver must still be able to resolve a recorded interval. `Battle::hold_principal_from_scoring` takes the winner from a finished run of the same mode and community count instead of a caller-supplied label, and the snapshot (every vault) is untouched until then.

Not done, so the matching tasks stay open:

- The module cannot verify that provider values really use the committed scales, finality or pools; it only validates the shape and identity of evidence the adapter hands it and cannot tell who produced it.
- There is no contract entry point yet. An authenticated adapter must build observations, call these functions and persist state; the round state is in memory only.
- Provider, cadence, boundary tolerance and quantization values remain OPEN policy decisions.
