# Lifecycle security review

This is a review of the lifecycle boundary in this branch, not an independent audit or a live integration certification.

- **Live acceptance remains blocked.** Audit approvals come from the whitelisted agent and backing approvals from the owner-trusted representation's own views, but neither is a proof of custody: a trusted representation that lies, or a compromised agent or owner, can still approve. The sandbox verification uses fixture claims, custody attestations and observations. Unknown audit policy and custody finality must not be treated as approval.
- **Backing approval path.** `refresh_lifecycle_backing` is agent-only with a deposit; the callback is private, never panics on a bad answer (it withdraws instead), refunds unused deposit and rolls back an unpaid write. The trust list is owner-only with one yocto. Residual risks: the owner chooses trusted representations; removal does not withdraw existing approvals; an approval lives 10 minutes and lock does not re-read the representation, so a deficit inside that window is missed until the next refresh.
- **Local token isolation.** Activation is one-way and requires no legacy battles; subsequent legacy proposals are rejected and the legacy USDC token cannot be a lifecycle representation. Sandbox fixtures verify that a late NEP-141 transfer is returned and that owner and worker cannot withdraw held principal using the legacy liquid-balance method. This does not establish foreign-chain recovery or backing reservation.
- **Storage and gas.** Battle and position creation and snapshot growth are charged to their callers; position credit updates fixed-size fields after registration. A 16-community sandbox fixture with bounded metadata consumed about 8.01 TGas for lock. The measurement is not a production gas guarantee. No position-storage cleanup or pre-lock cancellation policy is supplied.
- **State and authority.** Deposit accounting uses checked addition and is committed before returning from the receiver. Snapshot and recorded principal cannot be replaced after lock or by a conflicting winner. The fixture methods are compiled only with `sandbox-fixtures` and require a self-call; never enable this feature on a deployment. `lock_lifecycle_battle` is agent-only and requires an attached deposit of at least 1 yoctoNEAR in addition to the storage-cost check, so a function-call key cannot lock a battle, including by repeating a lock that costs no storage; the unused part is refunded.
- **Outstanding acceptance.** Live custody, live audit and live scoring are not verified. Neither a mock token nor the `PrincipalHold` accounting record proves source custody or disposition. The late-deposit refund destination (D17, issue #82) and DAO disposition remain open.

Validation includes Rust unit and workspace checks, NEAR sandbox fixture and fail-closed tests, OpenSpec validation, Node checks, and a Docker image build. See the PR for the exact results and any later CI changes.
