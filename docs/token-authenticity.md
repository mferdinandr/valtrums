## Manipulation risk in a 30-second battle, in plain terms

- A round scores two point observations 30 seconds apart. A wallet that owns most of the supply can sell or buy into a single pool at will, and in these pools a few thousand dollars already moves the price several percent. A pool of USD 49k (VLAD) or USD 111k (DEGEN) is cheap to push compared with a typical score gap.
- **VLAD**, 70%: one EOA (nonce 8,435, EIP-7702 delegated) holds 70% of supply. It is not a pool, locker or vesting contract, and no evidence shows who controls it. It can dump into a USD 49k pool, or buy back and wash-trade, at any time. Treat it as an unknown insider position.
- **DEGEN**, 88.2%: held by a 2-of-3 Gnosis Safe, so two signers must agree. That limits a surprise but it is still a team or treasury position, not a lock; nothing on chain prevents it from selling into the USD 111k pool. Market-cap-growth scoring is also distorted when 88% of supply never trades.
- Volume-to-market-cap scoring is the easiest to game on every token here: self-trading raises volume at the cost of fees. Price % gain and drawdown are moved by one large trade in a thin pool.
- CASHCAT, WALLET, GOOD and AI have no non-pool holder above 12.1% (largest non-pool wallet 12.1%, 2.7%, 7.2% and 2.6% respectively), so holder-driven manipulation is low; pool depth is the limit (GOOD about USD 129k, AI about USD 137k, both single-pool).

## Deviations / assumptions

- Chain reads came from keyless PublicNode, not the owner RPC, because the official host fails TLS (`CERT_HAS_EXPIRED`) and resets plain connections. State is latest only; the creation block is not derivable. The owner should re-run the on-chain section on the owner RPC or an archive node when it works.
- No Robinhood official token list, ecosystem page or docs could be read; whether any exists is open. CoinGecko onchain was not queried (key needed), so the CoinGecko ids are GeckoTerminal metadata only.
- "Authentic" here means independent aggregator or curated-list evidence agrees on the address; it does not prove the project is the real brand. Several brands (Robinhood Wallet, Vlad, Degen) have no on-chain owner that can be checked.
- **Address casing:** five of the six addresses in [reference-token-scan.md](reference-token-scan.md) and the issue are not valid EIP-55 checksums (CASHCAT is). Sourcify rejected them until lowercased; a strict validator (viem, ethers) will throw. Checksummed forms from Sourcify: WALLET `0x0339f5459FC690aC85F1782e15782A151b4A9E1b`, GOOD `0x5f62C57e5C537887117EeF828b7E3Ad41C009FEb`, AI `0x0d4a117DCCCE7bA3d0A475EC9068c20c35310FD8`, VLAD `0xfDBb27E971E72E38C41b1C772FB409C94dd92789`, DEGEN `0x0830A9Dd26a04e959657AB6788d45f5725590c32`. Decision for the owner: register the lowercase or checksummed form, not the mixed-case string in the docs. The same applies to the other candidate addresses above, apart from where noted.
- GeckoTerminal's USD liquidity is unreliable for new or low-holder tokens (USD 34.9M for a 18-holder DEGEN look-alike); I used DexScreener liquidity as the reference and quoted GeckoTerminal figures only as labelled. These figures also differ from the quote-side balances counted in the earlier scan.
- Options and trade-offs for the owner:
  - **Keep all six as chosen**: no change, but AI and VLAD carry the risks above, and a judge who searches the ticker may find the larger token.
  - **Replace AI and VLAD** with two of moon, HOODRAT, SANTACOIN: lower look-alike and concentration risk, but all three have their own caveat (tax, unverified source) and thinner or similar liquidity.
  - **Keep DEGEN with the documented risk** and decide whether a live token owner (`0x2448...1e9b`, not a Safe signer) is acceptable for a demo; replacing it with one of the candidates removes the 88% holder.
  - **Keep WALLET** only if showing the full address in the UI is acceptable; otherwise replace it, since the name cannot be told apart from three others.
- Not checked: whether the 12.1% CASHCAT wallet, the 70% VLAD wallet or the unnamed WIF contracts are related to the deployer (no transfer history without an archive node or indexer); the Safe signers' identities; ownership of the project websites.
