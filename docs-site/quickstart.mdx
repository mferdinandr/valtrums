This guide takes you from no account to a deposit in a live Valtrums battle. You need a NEAR testnet wallet with a little testnet NEAR for transaction fees. Everything else, including the tokens you play with, is free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Open the Valtrums app and select **Connect wallet** in the top bar. Choose a NEAR wallet and approve the connection on **testnet**.

    After you connect, **My balance** in the top bar shows your NEAR and token balances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim test tokens">
    Go to the **Faucet** page and select **Claim all**. Your wallet asks you to approve each claim. One approval covers up to two tokens.

    Your first claim of each token needs a small storage deposit. Unused funds are returned. You can claim each token again once its cooldown ends (once a day by default). See [Get test tokens](/guides/get-test-tokens).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a battle">
    Open **Arena** to see the battle list. Pick a battle with the status **Deposits open**. Its start time must still be in the future, because deposits close when the battle starts.

    No open battle? [Create one](/guides/create-a-battle). You choose the scoring mode and the start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your spot and deposit">
    On the battle page, find **Position & actions**:

    1. Choose the community (crew) you want to back.
    2. Select **Save your spot**. This registers your position.
    3. Enter an amount under **Deposit your tokens** and confirm in your wallet.

    Your first battle may ask for a one-time 0.05 NEAR storage prepayment, which you can withdraw later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the result">
    Your deposit shows as **In escrow** until the community's safety and backing approvals arrive, then as **Deposited**. At the start time the battle locks and the 30-second rounds begin. Stay on the battle page to watch the race and the round history.

    If your community wins, your prize appears on the **Portfolio** page. See [Claim your rewards](/guides/claim-rewards).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can do all of this in plain words too. Open **Assistant** and type, for example, `deposit 100 CASHCAT into the open arena`. See [The assistant](/tools/assistant).
</Tip>
