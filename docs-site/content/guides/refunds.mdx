A Valtrums battle needs at least two communities with deposits to start. If the start time passes with fewer than that, the battle can't lock. Anyone can then cancel it, and every depositor gets back exactly what was credited to them. No round is played and no fee is charged.

## When does a refund apply?

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Start time passed and fewer than two communities have deposits | The battle shows **NOT ENOUGH CREWS**. Anyone can cancel it, then depositors claim refunds. |
| Three communities joined, but one never got a deposit | No refund. The battle locks with the two funded communities, and the empty one doesn't play. |
| The leaders tie | No refund. The tied leaders play another round. |
| You deposited after the start time | The deposit isn't accepted and is returned. |

## Cancel and refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the battle">
    Open the battle page. In the open arena panel, the status reads **Not enough funded communities** or **Started, waiting for the lock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the battle">
    If it hasn't been cancelled yet, select **Cancel the battle** and confirm in your wallet. Anyone can do this once the start time has passed. The status changes to **Cancelled. Depositors can claim a refund.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your refund">
    Select **Refund**. If you have deposits in more than one community, the app refunds them one by one (**Refunding 1 of 2…**). When it's done, it says **Refunded. Check your wallet.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nothing is owed to you, the panel says **You have nothing to refund in this battle.** If a refund is interrupted, select **Continue refund**.

<Tip>
  Battles you can get a refund from appear under **Refundable** on your **Portfolio**. In the assistant, type `cancel battle 7`.
</Tip>
