Each Valtrums battle has its own page with a countdown, a race chart, the competing communities, your position and the round history. This page explains what you see at each stage and what each status means.

## Battle statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Deposits open** | The battle is gathering. You can join and deposit until the start time. |
| **Starting soon** / **GET READY** | The start time is close. |
| **LOCKING CREWS** | The start time has passed and the battle is being locked. Deposits are closed. |
| **Crews locked** | The communities and deposits are frozen. Rounds are about to begin. |
| **LIVE ROUND** | A 30-second round is running. The page shows the round number and time left. |
| **Tied · Overtime** | The leaders tied. Only they play another round. |
| **RESULT PENDING** | The round ended and Valtrums is waiting for the price data needed to score it. |
| **recorded winner** | A single winner exists and is stored by the arena. |
| **NOT ENOUGH CREWS** | Fewer than two communities had deposits at the start time. |
| **Cancelled** | The battle was cancelled. Depositors can claim a refund. |

## Before the battle starts

While a battle is gathering, the chart shows a **live price preview**: the reference prices of the competing tokens, as percent change since you opened the page.

<Note>
  The preview is for display only. It is not used for scoring. Rounds are scored only from the price observations Valtrums records after the battle locks. A token without a matching price pool isn't drawn, and a line stays flat while nobody trades.
</Note>

## During rounds

Once the crews are locked, the chart switches to the recorded round observations. Each round lasts exactly 30 seconds. **Round history** lists each round's candidates, their scores and who was eliminated.

If a round's data is incomplete, the round stays pending. Missing data never counts as zero and never decides a winner.

## Overtime

When two or more communities tie for the best score, the page shows **Tied · Overtime** and only the tied leaders play again. Every input resets. For example:

| Round | Price climb scores | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A +10%, B +10%, C +2% | A and B advance. C is eliminated, but its deposit stays locked. |
| 2 | A −3%, B −3% | Tie again. Another round. |
| 3 | A −4%, B −5% | A wins. B and C are both losing communities. |

There is no limit on overtime rounds. No new deposits are accepted while they run.

## When the battle ends

The winner is shown as the **recorded winner**. Settlement then splits each losing community's deposit. Check the **Portfolio** page for your prize, and see [Claim your rewards](/guides/claim-rewards). If your community won, you can also take part in [the winners' vote](/concepts/winners-vote).
