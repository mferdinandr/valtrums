After your community wins, your share of each losing deposit is recorded as a reward you can claim. You collect rewards on the **Portfolio** page, or on the battle page itself. Claimed rewards arrive in your NEAR wallet as the losing communities' tokens.

## When can I claim?

Rewards are recorded as soon as the battle is settled. The claim button opens once the [winners' vote](/concepts/winners-vote) on the winning deposit has finished. Until then, the battle page says **Claiming opens after the DAO decision.** and the Portfolio says a DAO decision is still running.

## Claim from the Portfolio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Portfolio">
    Go to **Portfolio**. The **Ready to claim** section lists what you can claim. If it's empty, it says "Nothing to claim yet. Prizes appear here after a battle is settled."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim all">
    Select **Claim all to my NEAR wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm each step">
    Approve each transaction in your wallet. The button shows progress, for example **Claiming 1 of 2…**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a battle page, the claim panel shows two kinds of rewards:

- **Prize from the losing community**: your share of each losing deposit.
- **Your coins that were not burned**: if your community won, the part of your own deposit that the winners' vote returned to you.

If settlement hasn't fully run yet, claiming finishes it first. The panel shows **Settling prizes (1 of 3)…** while it does.

## Reading your Portfolio

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Your positions** | Battles you joined, filterable by status: **Deposits open**, **In play**, **Refundable** |
| **Ready to claim** | Rewards you can claim now |
| **DAO decisions** | Winners' votes you can take part in, with "Your winning principal: burn or keep." |
| **Recovery alerts** | Late deposits or refunds that need an action from you |

If a section can't be read, the page says so instead of showing zero: "We can't check these rewards yet. Your balance is not necessarily zero."

<Tip>
  Ask the assistant `check my winnings` or `claim my winnings`. See [The assistant](/tools/assistant).
</Tip>
