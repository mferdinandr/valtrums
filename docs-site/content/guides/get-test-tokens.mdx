To play on Valtrums you need a NEAR testnet wallet and some of the supported community tokens. The Valtrums faucet gives out these tokens for free. They are test tokens on NEAR testnet with no value.

## Before you start

- A NEAR wallet that supports **testnet**.
- A little **testnet NEAR** in that wallet. Every claim and deposit is a transaction, and transactions need a small amount of NEAR for fees.

## Connect your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet menu">
    Select **Connect wallet** in the top bar of the Valtrums app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Choose your wallet and approve the connection on testnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balances">
    Select your account in the top bar to open **My balance**. It lists your NEAR balance and every supported token you hold. A new account shows **No balances yet.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your wallet only signs transactions you approve. Valtrums shows each transaction before your wallet asks you to sign it.

## Claim tokens from the faucet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the faucet">
    Go to the **Faucet** page. The **Your account** panel shows each token and whether it is **ready**, in **cooldown** or **unavailable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Select **Claim all**. The button shows how many tokens you can claim and how many wallet approvals that takes. One approval covers up to two tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in your wallet">
    Approve each transaction. Your first claim of each token also includes a small storage deposit. Unused funds are returned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each account can claim each token once per cooldown period. The page says "Claim community tokens once a day". If the faucet is paused or a token has run out for the day, the page shows **Paused.** or marks the token **unavailable**.
</Note>

## Which tokens can I play with?

Valtrums battles accept five community tokens: **CASHCAT**, **WALLET**, **GOOD**, **VLAD** and **DEGEN**. The AI token exists on testnet but isn't accepted in battles. The list of accepted tokens is managed by the Valtrums DAO. See [Token safety](/trust/token-safety).
