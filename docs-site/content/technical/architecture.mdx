Valtrums runs entirely on NEAR. The arena contract is the referee: it freezes each battle, scores every 30-second round from live on-chain market data, and splits the prize. Small workers feed it safety checks and prices, and none of them can touch player funds.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Arena** | Opens battles, locks them at the start time, scores rounds, handles ties, and settles rewards |
| **Community tokens** | One token contract per memecoin community. It keeps balances, rewards, escrow and burns in a single ledger that must always balance |
| **Safety checker** | Runs the pre-audit (a security scan plus a liquidity check) before a community can play |
| **Price collector** | Records point-in-time prices, supply and volume from the real tokens' on-chain markets |
| **Valtrums DAO** | Owns protocol settings such as fees and the join list; it cannot change a battle's result or vote |
| **Player agents** | NEAR AI agents that play with their own budget |
| **Assistant and web app** | Turn plain words into one reviewed action that you sign yourself |

## One battle, end to end

1. **Open.** A creator picks a scoring mode and a start time, then makes the first deposit.
2. **Join and deposit.** Communities join with memecoins that passed the pre-audit. A deposit counts once the community has a fresh safety approval and a backing approval.
3. **Lock.** At the start time the arena freezes the communities, vaults, mode and price policy. With fewer than two funded communities, the battle is cancelled and everyone is refunded.
4. **Rounds.** Each round lasts 30 seconds. The collector records prices and the arena checks the scores itself instead of trusting a worker's winner.
5. **Overtime.** If the leaders tie, they play again from scratch, with no limit on rounds.
6. **Settlement.** Every losing vault is split: 99.9% to the winning community by stake, 0.05% to the creator and 0.05% to the protocol.
7. **The winners' vote.** The winning community votes how much of its own deposit to burn or return. The arena contract counts the votes.
8. **Claim.** Winners claim their rewards into their own NEAR wallet.

## Design principles

- **The contract is the referee.** Scores, ties, fees and the vote are all computed on-chain with exact integer math.
- **Workers never hold your funds.** Safety checks and prices are evidence the arena verifies. Player funds stay in player accounts and token contracts.
- **Real data decides.** Rounds use live on-chain market data, never a made-up number, and missing data keeps a round pending instead of becoming a zero.
- **One ledger per token.** Rewards, refunds, escrow and burns come from the same balanced ledger, so the same tokens can never be paid twice.
- **The community decides its own deposit.** No owner, worker or DAO can override the winners' vote.
- **AI stays bounded.** Agents use their own budget, every decision comes from NEAR AI Cloud (TEE) and is logged for audit, and model output is treated as untrusted until validated.
