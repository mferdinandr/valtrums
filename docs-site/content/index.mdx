Valtrums is an open arena where token communities battle each other. You back a community by depositing its token. Communities then compete in 30-second rounds scored from real market prices. When a single winner remains, everyone who backed it shares the tokens that the losing communities deposited.

<Warning>
  Valtrums runs on **NEAR testnet**. The tokens you play with are test tokens with no value. No real money is involved.
</Warning>

## The problem

Memecoins live and die by one number: the price.

- **Only up and down.** A memecoin's value depends on the chart going up. When it goes down, holders have nothing else to hold on to.
- **No utility.** Most memecoins can't be used for anything. There is no game, prize, vote or purpose behind the token.
- **Falling prices leave nothing.** When the price drops, the token is just a bag. The community has no way to put it to work or get something back.
- **Trust is thin.** Honeypots, hidden taxes and thin markets make unchecked tokens risky, and the chain is hard for newcomers and AI agents to use.

## The solution

Valtrums gives memecoins something to do, whatever the chart says.

- **A use for every token.** Your memecoin becomes your community's stake in a battle: it can back a team, win prizes and join a vote, even when its price is down.
- **Real rules, real winners.** Communities compete in 30-second rounds scored from live on-chain market data. The contract decides the winner, with exact math and no operator.
- **Prizes in real tokens.** The winning community shares the losing communities' original tokens.
- **A mechanic with meaning.** The winners vote how much of their own deposit to burn, so a win can reshape the token's supply.
- **Safe and easy.** Every token passes a pre-audit before it plays, and the assistant and NEAR AI agents make it simple for everyone.

## How it works in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Someone opens a battle">
    Anyone can create a battle. The creator picks how the winner is scored and when the battle starts, then makes the first deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Communities join and deposit">
    Before the start time, anyone can bring in a community by depositing a memecoin that has passed the Valtrums pre-audit. On testnet you can start with **CASHCAT**, **WALLET**, **GOOD**, **VLAD** or **DEGEN**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rounds decide the winner">
    At the start time, deposits close and the battle locks. Each round lasts exactly 30 seconds. If the top communities tie, they play another round, as many times as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Winners share the losing tokens">
    The winning community's backers receive 99.9% of every losing community's deposit, split by how much each of them deposited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Winners vote on their own deposit">
    The winning community's own deposit is held aside. Its backers vote on how much of it to burn and how much to return to themselves.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes Valtrums different

- **You win real tokens, not points.** Rewards are the original tokens the losing communities deposited. They are not swapped into another token.
- **Real prices decide.** Every round is scored from live on-chain market data of the real tokens.
- **Unsafe tokens are kept out.** A community can only play after its token passes a safety check and a liquidity check. See [Token safety](/trust/token-safety).
- **The community decides.** The winners, not an operator, vote on what happens to their own deposit. See [The winners' vote](/concepts/winners-vote).
- **AI agents can play for you.** You can fund an agent that picks battles with NEAR AI. See [Player agents](/tools/player-agents).
