Everything you deposit, win and claim in Valtrums stays on **NEAR**. Each community token is its own NEAR token contract, and the arena contract holds deposits while a battle runs. This page explains where your tokens are at each stage and what protects them.

## Where your tokens are

| Stage | Where the tokens are |
| --- | --- |
| Before you play | In your NEAR wallet, after you claim them from the [faucet](/guides/get-test-tokens) |
| After you deposit | Held by the arena contract for that battle |
| Battle cancelled | Back to you as a refund you claim. See [Get a refund](/guides/refunds). |
| Your community lost | Shared among the winners as rewards |
| Your community won | Your own deposit waits for [the winners' vote](/concepts/winners-vote); your prize from the losers is recorded as a reward |
| You claim | In your NEAR wallet again |

## How prizes and burns are recorded

- **Losing deposits become rewards.** At settlement, the losing communities' tokens leave the arena and are recorded in each token contract as rewards for the winners. When you claim, the same amount arrives in your NEAR wallet.
- **The winning deposit waits for the vote.** It is set aside in escrow and can't be moved by anyone until the vote is decided.
- **Returned tokens** become rewards you claim like any other prize.
- **Burned tokens** are destroyed on NEAR. They leave the token's supply and are never created again.

## Every unit is accounted for

- **Only real deposits count.** You are credited only for the tokens the arena actually receives.
- **Nothing is counted twice.** The same tokens can't be a reward, a refund and a burn at the same time.
- **The totals must add up.** Each token contract keeps a ledger of tokens in wallets, rewards waiting to be claimed, deposits in escrow and burns, and checks that it balances.
- **The faucet never creates tokens.** It only hands out tokens it already holds.

## Who can do what

| Part | Its job | What it can't do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arena contract | Runs battles, scores rounds, splits rewards, counts votes | Doesn't trust any worker's claim about who won. It checks the scores itself. |
| Token contracts | Hold balances, rewards, escrow and burns for each community token | Can't create rewards without a settled battle |
| Safety checker | Approves or rejects tokens | Missing data stays Pending |
| Price collector | Records round prices from the real tokens' markets on Robinhood Chain | Never fills in missing data |
| Valtrums DAO | Changes protocol settings such as fees and the allowlist | Can't change or veto a battle's vote |
| Player agents | Play within a budget you fund | Have no access to your own wallet |

<Note>
  Valtrums' security notes are a self-review by the development team, not an external audit. Valtrums runs on NEAR testnet only.
</Note>
