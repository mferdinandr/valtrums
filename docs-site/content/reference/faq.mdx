Quick answers to the questions Valtrums players ask most. Each answer links to the page with the full details.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Valtrums use real money?">
    No. Valtrums runs on NEAR testnet, and the tokens are test tokens with no value. Get them for free from the [faucet](/guides/get-test-tokens).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my deposit say 'In escrow'?">
    Your community is still waiting for its safety and backing approvals. Your deposit counts as soon as both are in. See [Join and deposit](/guides/join-and-deposit#why-does-my-deposit-say-in-escrow).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app says 'Partly credited'. Did I lose tokens?">
    The arena credited less than you sent. The rest was refunded or is still on its way. Check your wallet balance and **Recovery alerts** on your **Portfolio**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add more after the battle starts?">
    No. Joining and deposits close at the start time and stay closed through every round, including overtime. A late deposit is returned.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the communities tie?">
    Only the tied leaders play another 30-second round, with every input reset. This repeats until one community wins. See [How a battle works](/concepts/how-a-battle-works#5-overtime).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if only one community deposited?">
    The battle can't start. After the start time anyone can cancel it, and every depositor gets back exactly what they deposited. See [Get a refund](/guides/refunds).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My community lost. Do I get anything back?">
    No. The losing deposit goes to the winners (99.9%), the creator (0.05%) and the protocol (0.05%). See [Rewards and fees](/concepts/rewards-and-fees).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My community won. Do I get my own deposit back?">
    That depends on [the winners' vote](/concepts/winners-vote). The winning backers decide what share is burned and what share is returned. If nobody votes, the deposit stays in escrow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I claim my prize yet?">
    Claiming opens after the winners' vote has finished. Your prize is already recorded, so it won't be lost while you wait. See [Claim your rewards](/guides/claim-rewards).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a player agent spend my wallet's funds?">
    No. An agent only uses the tokens in its own account, which you fund. See [Player agents](/tools/player-agents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI in the assistant sign anything?">
    No. The assistant only prepares an action. You review it and sign it in your wallet. See [The assistant](/tools/assistant).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transactions fail or the page is slow. What can I do?">
    Public NEAR testnet services sometimes limit requests. Wait a moment and try again. Make sure you have a little testnet NEAR for fees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
